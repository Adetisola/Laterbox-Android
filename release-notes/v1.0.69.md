## Highlights
- Added public Android emulator compatibility for Movies & TV catalogue discovery, search, playback, and in-app updates while preserving Play Integrity verification on supported Android devices.

## What's Improved
- Public Android Emulator Support: Laterbox installations on PC Android emulators (such as LDPlayer) now automatically establish a secure guest session when Google Play Integrity attestation is unavailable, enabling full catalogue browsing, search, stream resolution, and remote configuration sync without manual token or setting changes.
- In-App Updater Compatibility: Checking for updates and downloading release assets now authenticate seamlessly across both verified Android devices and emulator guest sessions.
- Sanitized Catalogue Fallback Notices: When the primary catalogue service is temporarily unreachable or busy, Laterbox displays clear, user-friendly status notices while falling back to open archive and TV guide content.

## Bug Fixes
- Fixed an issue where PC emulator installations could receive an authentication error when checking for updates or downloading new releases in-app.
- Fixed an issue where primary Movies & TV catalogue rows and search results were missing on Android emulators lacking Play Integrity attestation.
