## Highlights
- Unified app-wide toast notifications with persistent offline status, cleaner Movies & TV discovery cards and Surprise Me controls, and a calmer Assistant conversation workspace.

## What's Improved
- Unified Notifications & Offline Status: Feedback and error notices across screens now render through a consistent elevated toast banner above the navigation bar and keyboard, alongside a persistent "You're offline" banner that automatically clears once validated internet returns.
- Movies & TV Refinement: Standardized TV episode card artwork using rich episode stills, prioritized primary streams over duplicate entries across discovery rails, refreshed the Surprise Me card buttons, and removed the redundant floating play icon overlay from browse poster cards.
- Assistant Workspace Clarity: Moved assistant details cleanly into the top menu, enabled per-message text selection across Markdown formatting, and added compact Copy and safe Retry quick actions below replies.
- Unified Update Prompts: Version-policy prompts and routine release updates now share a single clean modal with clear required versus optional actions.

## Bug Fixes
- Prevented repeated retry prompts in Assistant chat from targeting the wrong turn or re-running irreversible library actions.
- Prevented temporary network or server errors from falsely triggering the device offline banner when internet connectivity remains validated.
