## Highlights
- Introduced edge-to-edge transparent Android gesture navigation, a simplified three-category storage overview, optional video player Ambient Mode, and direct episode-level quick downloads.

## What's Improved
- Transparent Gesture Navigation: Content across player screens, bottom sheets, and settings now extends seamlessly behind the Android system gesture bar with crisp icon contrast and safe touch clearance in both gesture and three-button navigation modes.
- Simplified Data & Storage: Redesigned Settings storage usage into three clear categories (`Downloaded`, `Cache`, and `App data`) with an animated stacked indicator bar and direct access to cache cleanup and downloads.
- Video Player Ambient Mode: Added an optional, toggleable Ambient Mode in the player controls that gently illuminates letterboxed areas with colors sampled from the active video and remembers your preference across sessions.
- Quick Episode Downloads: Added an episode-level download button with a compact horizontal resolution selector popover, live progress indicator, and inline pause, resume, retry, and completion states.
- Player & Queue Clarity: Highlighted the active subtitle track with offline badges for local subtitle files and smoothed download queue progress indicators.

## Bug Fixes
- Fixed an issue where bottom sheets and settings screens could sit too close to system navigation bars on edge-to-edge devices.
- Fixed an issue where queued offline analytics batches could briefly leave stale items behind after permanent server rejection.
