# What's New in Laterbox v1.0.86

### Smoother Inbox, Archive & Collections Scrolling
- **Stable Hero cards:** Locked Hero cover images to a consistent 16:9 frame with steady placeholders so loading or unavailable images never resize cards or cause scroll jumps mid-fling.
- **Preserved scroll position:** Switching between Hero, Compact, and Editorial views or navigating between Inbox, Archive, and Collections now keeps your scroll position anchored smoothly without snapping back.
- **Quieter list updates:** Background link enrichment banners no longer shift the sticky header height while scrolling.

### Movies, TV & Player Refinements
- **Synced ambient lighting:** Rebuilt video player ambient lighting to sample decoded frames cleanly in the background with tighter frame timing and faster, more responsive color transitions.
- **Playable dub fallback & English captions:** When an original language stream is unavailable for a title, Laterbox automatically falls back to an available dub, notifies you cleanly, and attaches English subtitles when needed.
- **Cleaner detail layout & faster playback startup:** Streamlined Movies & TV metadata, audio track, and subtitle selectors into horizontally scrollable divider rows, added a 2-line expandable synopsis, improved stacked subtitle rendering, verified local files before showing downloaded episode checkmarks, and sped up cold-open playback readiness.

### Assistant, Settings & Reliability
- **Inline assistant approvals & voice controls:** Sensitive assistant actions now request confirmation cleanly inside the composer with optional per-chat Always Allow permissions, paired with separate Stop and Send dictation controls and accurate local time awareness.
- **Pinned Settings headers & accurate sessions:** Settings headers remain visible while scrolling, scheduled sleep/usage timers enforce accurately on device, and foreground transitions record active app sessions reliably.
