## Highlights
- Added hierarchical series and season grouping in Viewing History, improved offline connectivity accuracy, restored missing Storage Overview details in Data & Storage, and refined player Ambient Mode lighting.

## What's Improved
- Organized Viewing History: Multi-episode TV series now group neatly under series and season folders while keeping movies, trailers, and single-episode watches flat at the top level.
- Refined Ambient Mode: Slightly increased Ambient Mode glow prominence in the video player for a richer atmospheric backdrop without sacrificing playback clarity or HUD readability.
- Cleaner Data & Storage Settings: Focused Backup & Restore on reliable manual JSON export and import while keeping storage management streamlined.

## Bug Fixes
- Fixed missing Storage Overview content under Settings -> Data & Storage so storage breakdown, visual category usage, and safe cache controls load automatically with clear loading and retry states.
- Improved offline status detection so the offline notice only triggers when your device truly lacks validated internet access, updates reliably when returning to the app, and avoids brief flashes while switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data.
