## Highlights
- Introduces on-device Experience Intelligence across Movies & TV, Inbox, Collections, Search, and Assistant with full local transparency controls and expanded backups.

## What's Improved
- Personalization & Privacy Controls: Added a new Personalization & Privacy section in Settings to inspect inferred interests, pin or mute topics, pause personalization, choose whether to share taste context with Assistant, or reset the local model in one tap without touching your saved links or watchlist.
- Personalized Movies & TV Discovery: Added a Picked For You rail with diverse recommendations, "Why this?" explanations, and quick shortcuts to tune your tastes.
- Smarter Inbox, Collections & Search: Added a dismissible Pick Back Up strip for relevant saved links while keeping chronological order intact, 1-tap collection suggestions on uncategorized items, Collections search filtering, and lexical-first search tie-breaking.
- Expanded Backup & Restore: Backups now include your taste preferences and Movies & TV watchlist alongside saved links, collections, and scheduled reminders, while remaining compatible with older backup files.
- Truthful Assistant & Notification Governance: Improved Assistant capability awareness, step-by-step confirmation handling, and calm proactive notification spacing without ever delaying your scheduled reminders.

## Bug Fixes
- Automatically reschedules future link reminder alarms when restoring a backup and cleans up legacy alarm schedules on upgrade.
- Stops dependent Assistant actions cleanly when an earlier step is declined or fails, improves fallback chat title formatting for multi-part domains, and prevents Clear All notification swipes from throttling helpful reminders.
- Fixes Movies & TV reminder open counting and prevents false Assistant feature-update banners for guest sessions and older app versions.
