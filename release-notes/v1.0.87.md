# What's New in Laterbox v1.0.87

### Always-On Experience Intelligence & Simpler Settings
- **Natural learning across the app:** Laterbox now adapts continuously on-device from your everyday actions—watching, liking or disliking titles, downloading, sharing, saving to collections, searching, and clearing Continue Watching—without a separate personalization setup screen.
- **Content-aware Movies & TV discovery:** Added thumbs-up and thumbs-down feedback alongside richer taste matching across genres, cast, and creators to keep Home shelves and recommendations tuned to what you actually enjoy.
- **Assistant taste context & backup support:** Added a dedicated Assistant Taste Context toggle in Reminders & AI settings to control whether taste preferences are shared with assistant replies, and preserved your local intelligence profile cleanly across backups and restores.

### Movies, TV & Detail Layout Refinements
- **Focused two-row detail controls:** Simplified the area below the Play button into two smoothly scrolling faded rows—concise metadata text chips and pill-shaped audio track selectors—while removing the extra subtitle row.
- **Reliable native captions:** Restored native built-in subtitle behavior during playback without unexpected fallback overrides.

### Stacked Subtitles & Responsive Ambient Lighting
- **Multi-line subtitle stacking:** Simultaneous dialogue cues now stack vertically instead of overlapping, with increased bottom safe-area clearance while keeping your chosen subtitle font size, colors, and background styling untouched.
- **Faster, smoother ambient glow:** Tightened ambient lighting frame sampling intervals with smooth linear color transitions offloaded from the main thread so the player backdrop responds quickly to scene cuts without flicker or frame drops.
