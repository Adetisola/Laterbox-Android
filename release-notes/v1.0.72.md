## Highlights
- Made video player Ambient Mode faster and more vivid in sync with scene changes, and added 100-episode range navigation for the One Piece anime on both the detail page and download selector.

## What's Improved
- More Responsive Ambient Mode: Increased Ambient Mode color glow subtly and synchronized live frame sampling closely with playback, seeks, speed changes, and quick bright-to-dark scene cuts so the surrounding glow reacts naturally without lagging behind the video.
- One Piece 100-Episode Navigation: Added horizontally scrollable 100-episode range chips (such as 1–100, 101–200, and Specials) for the classic One Piece anime on both the title detail episode list and the episode download sheet, keeping your selected download episodes intact when switching between ranges.

## Bug Fixes
- Fixed noticeable delay when Ambient Mode transitioned between high-brightness and dark video scenes, while preserving deep darkness during dark scenes.
- Kept episode navigation cleanly scoped so other series and unrelated One Piece titles continue using their standard episode layout.
