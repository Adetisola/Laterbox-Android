## Highlights
- Added native Android Picture-in-Picture support for active video playback, complete with quick-seek remote actions synced to your seek-interval preference, alongside a unified across-the-board UI refinement and Remote Control Plane support.

## What's Improved
- Added a contextual floating quick-action dock across Inbox, Collections, and Movies & TV for faster access to common actions.
- Standardized primary tab headers, icons, and confirmation prompts for destructive actions across downloads and saved content.
- Grouped TV series downloads by series and season in Downloads for cleaner library organization.
- Consolidated storage management into Data & Storage, moved About details directly into Settings, and simplified privacy and backup notices.
- Added remote configuration sync, maintenance notices, and feature-flag support so app capabilities can update seamlessly.

## Bug Fixes
- Removed the duplicate rotation lock button in the movie player and fixed movie and TV series watched-status toggles.
