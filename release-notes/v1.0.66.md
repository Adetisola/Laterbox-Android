## Highlights
- App-wide maintenance status bar, cleaner update prompts across screens, curated Movies & TV spotlight banners, playback notification artwork, and reliable Picture-in-Picture dismissal.

## What's Improved
- Service Status & Multi-Screen Notices: Added a persistent top scrolling strip for maintenance announcements and normal-mode service notices, alongside app-wide floating notices and recommended update prompts with clear priority ordering.
- Movies & TV Showcase: Added support for curated visual slides and smoother rotation in the Movies & TV hero carousel.
- Playback Notifications: Added the monochrome Laterbox notification icon and automatic poster, episode still, or saved local thumbnail artwork for playback controls, with a branded fallback when no artwork is available.
- Offline Resilience During Maintenance: Ensured saved links, collections, reader articles, and downloaded media continue working offline when online streaming or AI services are paused for maintenance.

## Bug Fixes
- Fixed Picture-in-Picture window dismissal leaving background audio or the foreground playback service running when system lifecycle callbacks arrive out of order.
- Removed redundant per-screen banners that overlapped list content across Inbox and Movies & TV screens.
